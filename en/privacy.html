Privacy policy

Version of 13 November 2026

Who publishes Algoo

Aion Digital Products — Tony Domineaux, French sole proprietor (see the legal notice). Contact: contact@helloalgoo.app.

Your journal stays on your devices

What you note is saved on your iPhone and, if you use it, on your Apple Watch. There is no account and no Algoo server: the publisher has no access to your journal. The app contains no advertising and no analytics.

When your data leaves the app

  • Between iPhone and Apple Watch, your entries pass from one device to the other.
  • An export (PDF report or data files) is shared only when you ask, with the recipient you choose.
  • The journal may be part of your device backups, depending on your Apple settings.

Apple Health

The connection is optional. If you allow it, Algoo reads the data types you choose when preparing a report, and never writes to Health. This data appears in a separate section of the report; it is not copied into the journal and is never used for advertising. You can withdraw permission at any time in the Health app.

Subscription

Purchases are handled by Apple. Algoo only receives the status of your subscription, never your payment details.

Keeping and deleting

You can delete an entry from the journal, with or without a subscription. Uninstalling the app erases the journal from that device. Copies on Apple Watch or in a device backup follow their own settings.

If you write to us

Your message and email address are used only to reply to you. Please do not send health information or screenshots of your journal. You can ask to access, correct or delete your messages at the contact address, and lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL (cnil.fr).

This website

This site sets no cookies and contains no forms. Like any web host, ours may keep technical connection logs.
